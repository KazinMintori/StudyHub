A note on variables

There are three things that “variable” could be referring to:

a phenomenon

how the phenomenon is being recorded or measured into data

what values can it take? (this is often an intent- or value-laden exercise!)

for numerical units, what unit should we express it in?

How the recorded data is being analyzed

might you bin/discretizing income data? what are the consequences of this?

Images as data

Images are composed of pixels (this image is 1520 by 1012)

The color in each pixel is in RGB

Each band takes a value from 0-255

This image is data with 1520 x 1012 x 3 values.

At this point, it might help them to draw a rectangle with cells, or an array and put three numbers in a few cells, to indicate what we are talking about. Later, when you talk about grayscale, you could erase the three numbers and put just 1 number. Numbers you could use (starting at top left corner and moving across top row) for rgb: (153, 159, 155), (170, 176, 173), (182, 189, 185), and a grass green from the bottom row could be (82, 99, 28). In the grayscale picture, these 4 transform to 156, 174, 187, 59, In the first 8x8 picture, the values are 112, 162, 196 for first three pixels.

Grayscale

Grayscale images have only one band

0 is black, 255 is white

This image is data with 1520 x 1012 x 1 values.
