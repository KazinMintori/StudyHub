Are we describing a probability distribution below that is binomial? Or is it hypergeometric? Or do neither apply?

A bag that has 6 pieces of fruit: 2 mangoes, 3 apples, and 1 orange. I reach into the bag and draw out one fruit at a time, selecting each fruit at random (so all the fruit left in the bag are equally likely on each draw). I put the fruit away after I take it out (and don’t put it back into the bag). I count the number of draws until and including the first time I draw a apple.

Binomial, hypergeometric, or neither?

Neither. It would be useful at this point to write out what is going on, and point out that they are using the multiplication rule and a conditional probability.
